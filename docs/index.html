How it works

  1. IngestFour file formats are read onto one minute-by-minute timeline.
  2. Template the logsIDs and numbers are masked so identical sentences collapse together.
  3. Score against normalA robust z-score from the median and MAD, after removing the day and night cycle.
  4. Group into incidentsSignals join when they are close in time and connected in the dependency graph.
  5. Rank the causesDeploys and services are scored, and the arithmetic is shown for each.
  6. Match earlier incidentsSymptom sets are compared so a repeat points back to its first occurrence.
